# Privacy Policy — PasteShelf

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

PasteShelf is a clipboard manager, so by design it sees what you copy. Here is exactly what happens with it.

- **Stays on your Mac.** Your clipboard history is saved only in `~/Library/Application Support/PasteShelf/`.
  It is never uploaded, synced, or shared.
- **No network access.** The app never connects to the internet.
- **No analytics or tracking.** There are no third-party SDKs.
- **Sensitive data is skipped.** Items marked as concealed or transient by password managers and other apps
  (see <http://nspasteboard.org>) are never stored.
- **You're in control.** Delete any item, clear the whole history, or delete the folder above at any time.
- **Accessibility permission** is used only to send a ⌘V keystroke to the app you were using, right after you pick an item.
  PasteShelf does not read what's on your screen or what you type.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/sonzini/PasteShelf/issues>.
